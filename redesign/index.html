Why it matters

Air pollution causes 1 in 9 deaths worldwide

The World Health Organization attributes about seven million premature deaths every year to air pollution — 4.2 million from ambient (outdoor) pollution and 3.8 million from household air. Yet across much of Africa, the street-level data needed to act barely exists. sensors.AFRICA closes that gap with open data anyone can interrogate.

36% of lung-cancer deaths are linked to air pollution
35% of deaths from COPD are linked to air pollution

How it works

A weather station for pollution, on your wall

1 Get a sensor We use open-source Luftdaten technology built around the SDS011 particulate sensor — cheap enough for a household, accurate enough for a newsroom.
2 Mount it & connect Fix it outside a window or on a balcony, connect it to Wi-Fi, and it starts measuring PM2.5 and PM10 in the air around your home.
3 Your data goes public Readings stream to the open sensors.AFRICA platform — mapped, archived and free for journalists, researchers and neighbours to use.

Your city can't fix what it can't see.

Host a sensor at your home, school or office and put your street on the map. No lab coat required — just a window ledge and Wi-Fi.
