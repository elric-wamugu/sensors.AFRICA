Why sound, why now

Noise is the pollution everyone hears and no one measures

Noise is more than a nuisance. Sustained exposure is linked to chronic stress, sleep disturbance, poorer concentration in schoolchildren and elevated cardiovascular risk — the World Health Organization ranks environmental noise among the top environmental burdens on health in the regions where it has been studied. In western Europe alone, WHO estimates traffic noise costs more than a million healthy life-years every year.

African cities are among the most vibrant — and often the loudest — on earth, yet almost none of that exposure is measured, and even less of it is public. When a resident complains about the generator next door or the new flight path overhead, there is rarely a number to point to. This stream will put one on the record: calibrated, citizen-hosted noise levels, mapped block by block and published as open data — measuring levels, not conversations.
